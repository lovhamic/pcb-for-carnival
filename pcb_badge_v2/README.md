# Space Carnival PCB Badge v2 - Echoes of Years

40 x 40 mm, 2-layer. 8 x 3 mm LEDs on the Ferris-wheel joints (top, UL, UR, L, R, hub, LL, LR; bottom joint hidden by the tent is skipped).
One shared 68R resistor, CR2032 + through-hole holder on the back.
Folders: kicad/, gerbers/ (+drill), schematic/, bom/, render/, scripts/.
Fab: 2 layers, 1.6 mm, white mask, black silkscreen (the silk is the artwork).
Verify before ordering: holder footprint vs the real part, LED (D8) joints vs holder body, silk fine detail in the fab preview.
